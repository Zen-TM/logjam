# Code of conduct

Logjam is a small project with one maintainer. The rules for taking part in
its issues, pull requests and discussions are short:

- Be respectful. Criticise the work, not the person.
- No harassment, personal attacks, or sexual or discriminatory language or
  imagery.
- Never post someone else's private information, including the places they
  keep in Logjam.

The maintainer may edit or remove anything that breaks these rules, and may
block anyone who keeps breaking them.

To report something privately, email
[zentmarcos@gmail.com](mailto:zentmarcos@gmail.com?subject=Logjam%20conduct)
with "Logjam conduct" in the subject.
