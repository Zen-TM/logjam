Logjam — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-03

Logjam is built as a private tool. Privacy is a design constraint, not a feature, because exposing canyon locations causes real environmental harm and can compromise rescue and access agreements. This policy explains what we store, why, and what we deliberately do not do.

Logjam is intended for users in Australia. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

What we store

  • Account data: username, email, sign-in credentials (via AWS Cognito), theme and UI preferences, storage usage.
  • Canyon data: name, coordinates, grades, notes, attributes, and any media you upload.
  • Trip logs: dates, notes, custom fields, and media attached to your own trips.
  • Sharing: which of your friends each canyon is shared with.
  • Topo jobs: LiDAR inputs you upload and the rendered map tiles produced from them.
  • Operational logs: request error logs and worker job state. These are scrubbed so canyon names and coordinates do not appear in plain text.

What we do not do

  • No third-party analytics, advertising, or telemetry leaving your account.
  • No public or unauthenticated endpoints on user data — every API route requires authentication.
  • No default public sharing. Sharing is explicit, per-canyon, between authenticated users you have a friendship with.
  • No selling, renting, or monetising your data.

How sharing works

When you share a canyon with another Logjam user, that user can see the canyon record including its name, coordinates, canyon-level notes and canyon-level media. Per-trip notes, per-trip media, and your trip-log history remain private to you.

Where data is hosted

Logjam runs on AWS in the ap-southeast-2 (Sydney) region. Photos, topo input, and rendered tiles are stored in S3. The database is RDS PostgreSQL. Email goes via Amazon SES. Authentication is Amazon Cognito.

Email

We email you only for:

  • Account verification and password reset codes.
  • Notification when a topo job you started has finished.

No marketing email is ever sent.

Your controls

  • Export: contact us if you want a copy of your data.
  • Delete: the Account panel has a "Delete account" button that permanently removes every record we hold for you, including canyons, trip logs, media, friendships, shares, and notifications.
  • Access: you can request a machine-readable copy of the personal information we hold about you, provided as a JSON export.
  • Correct: you can edit your username, email, canyon records, and trip logs at any time, and can ask us to correct any other personal information that is inaccurate.

Data breaches

If a data breach occurs that is likely to result in serious harm, we will assess it and notify both the affected users and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) as required under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

Complaints

If you have a privacy concern, contact us first at the address below and we will respond. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

Retention

Data is retained until you delete it or the account. Operational logs may persist in AWS CloudWatch for up to 90 days after the originating record is removed.

Children

Logjam is not directed at children under 16. Do not create an account for a minor.

Changes to this policy

When this policy materially changes, the "Last updated" date above will change and you will be asked to re-consent on next sign-in.
