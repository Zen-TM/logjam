Last updated: 2026-05-21

You are responsible for the security of your sign-in credentials. One person per account. You must provide a real, working email address so we can deliver verification codes and account notifications.

2. Acceptable use

Logjam exists to help canyoners record and privately share trip information. You agree not to use the service to:

We follow guidance from NSW NPWS: "Be mindful not to publicise 'new' canyons or routes, particularly those in wilderness areas, to preserve opportunities for discovery and to minimise environmental impacts." Sharing on Logjam is explicit and per-canyon, between authenticated users only.

You are solely responsible for your own safety decisions. Logjam is provided "as is" without any warranty of fitness for trip planning, navigation, or rescue.

You can delete your account at any time from the Account panel. Deletion permanently removes your canyons, trip logs, media, notifications, friendships, and shares. This action cannot be undone.

Logjam is offered without uptime guarantees. We may suspend or terminate accounts that violate these terms, particularly those that publicise shared location data. Where reasonable, we will give notice via your account email.
