Logjam — Delete your account

This page is for Logjam GPS, the Android phone app, and Logjam Web at logjamnsw.com, both operated by Zen Marcos. They share one account, so deleting it in either deletes it in both. Deleting it cannot be undone.

Delete your account in Logjam GPS

  1. Open Logjam GPS and sign in. It needs a connection to delete.
  2. Tap More, then Account.
  3. Under “Leaving”, tap Delete account.
  4. Type the phrase it shows: “delete” and your username, such as delete sam.
  5. Tap Delete my account. Logjam GPS signs you out and clears its copy of your data from the phone.

Delete your account in Logjam Web

  1. Sign in at logjamnsw.com.
  2. Open Account in the sidebar (on a narrow screen, More, then Account).
  3. Under “Leaving”, select Delete account.
  4. Type the phrase it shows: “delete” and your username, such as delete sam.
  5. Select Delete account. You are signed out.

If you cannot sign in

Email zentmarcos@gmail.com from the address your account uses, with your username, and ask for it to be deleted. We may ask you to confirm the account is yours first.

What is deleted

All of this goes when you confirm, not later:

  • Your sign-in: username, email address and password.
  • Your places, routes, recorded tracks, trip logs, notes and custom fields.
  • Your photos, videos and other files, including files you sent to friends, and your topo jobs, maps and area exports.
  • Your friends list, the shares you made or received, your notifications, and the phones registered for notifications.
  • Notifications other people hold about your shares, friend requests and files you sent.
  • Your place in trip plans and your answers to their forms.

What is kept

  • Copies other people made: a place a friend copied from what you shared, or a file they accepted from you, stays theirs.
  • Trip plans that have other people in them: what you added stays for them, no longer linked to you, and a plan you made stays with them too. A plan with no one else in it is deleted with you.
  • Invite links you made to a trip plan work until they expire (7 days) or a leader turns them off.
  • Request logs and similar records, which include IP addresses, are not removed with your account and expire after 14 days.
  • The write-once audit logs of operator access and of each upload and download of your files are not removed with your account either and expire after 365 days. The Privacy Policy lists what each log holds.

Backups and deleted files

Backups of our database, and copies of photos and files you deleted, are kept for up to 10 days after you delete them, then are gone.
